# Volunteer participation policy

Aniob welcomes students and developers who want to learn, collaborate, and contribute to a growing software project. Contributors can work on practical engineering challenges, strengthen technical skills, build a portfolio, and receive recognition for accepted contributions.

Participation is voluntary. Any additional opportunities, if available, will be communicated separately.

## Taking part

- Choose an open issue, ask questions, and contribute through your own fork and pull request. No application form or direct write access is needed.
- Keep changes focused, communicate if the scope changes, and follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), security guidance, and project conventions.
- Submit work you have the right to contribute. Identify third-party code or assets and their terms.
- If a separate contribution agreement applies to work intended for the product, the founder will explain it clearly before that work begins. See [IP_POLICY.md](IP_POLICY.md).

## Recognition

Accepted contributions are acknowledged in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` or release notes, respecting the contributor's preferred public attribution. GitHub contribution history may also recognize work. Any additional recognition opportunities, if available, will be communicated separately.

## Taking a break or leaving

Participation is flexible; you can pause or stop at any time. You may close an unmerged PR or ask the founder to close it. Any signed contribution agreement continues to apply according to its terms.

## Contact

For ordinary coordination, comment on the issue or PR. For a private concern, contact [Revanth Boina](https://github.com/RevanthBoina). Report vulnerabilities using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public discussion.

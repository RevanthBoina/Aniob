# Contribution ownership and intellectual property policy

Aniob is a public personal repository owned and controlled by founder Revanth Boina. Its commercial-product direction does not make the code open source: no project-level open-source license is currently included. Repository visibility, a fork, an issue comment, a pull request, or access to source code **does not by itself transfer copyright or grant rights to use, commercialize, or redistribute a contribution**.

## Keep these work categories separate

- **Founder-created work:** work authored by Revanth Boina or otherwise owned/licensed by the founder remains subject to its existing rights and notices. This policy does not remove or change them.
- **Contributor-created work:** copyright initially belongs to its author under applicable law unless a valid separate agreement says otherwise. Submitting a PR does not assign it.
- **Pre-existing contributor work:** code, assets, or other materials created before a contribution remain the contributor's unless a separate agreement expressly covers them. Do not include them unless you have authority and clearly identify them.
- **Third-party material/dependencies:** each library, model, dataset, font, image, or copied snippet keeps its own author and license/terms. Identify and verify it; a contribution agreement cannot give Aniob rights the contributor does not have.

## How accepted contributions are handled

The founder reviews contributions and may accept, request revisions, or decline them. **Before accepted volunteer work is merged or incorporated into the commercial product, the founder and contributor must separately agree in writing to the applicable contribution terms, including any agreed copyright assignment.** The scope and terms must be shared clearly with the contributor; do not treat GitHub's PR interface or this policy as the agreement. A PR may be discussed and reviewed before then, but repository access or review does not change ownership.

A contributor should not sign or accept terms they do not understand. The founder should obtain appropriate advice and confirm the contributor has authority to enter any agreement. No agreement is signed on behalf of either party through this repository policy.

## Founder checklist (handled separately)

Before product incorporation of volunteer work, the founder should:

1. Arrange a concise contribution agreement covering only the intended contribution and the agreed rights (including assignment only where expressly agreed).
2. Explain the terms in plain language and make sure the contributor can ask questions and keep a copy.
3. Check age/capacity and any applicable institutional, employer, sponsor, or third-party rights constraints; obtain appropriate legal advice, especially for minors or cross-border contributions.
4. Record the relevant PR/commit and the signed agreement securely, without publishing personal contact data.
5. Review third-party dependencies, model/data terms, attribution, and existing copyright/license notices separately.

This is an informational project policy, not legal advice or a contribution agreement. Do not request sensitive identity documents, Aadhaar, PAN, passwords, or unnecessary personal data as part of ordinary onboarding.

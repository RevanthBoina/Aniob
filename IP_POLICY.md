# Contribution ownership policy

Aniob is a founder-led commercial project controlled by Revanth Boina. Rights in founder-created work remain with the founder, subject to any existing notices or third-party rights. The repository is public, but it has no project-level open-source licence. Public access or submitting a Pull Request does not transfer copyright or other ownership.

## Contributor work and the agreement

Joining the community, discussing an issue, or opening a PR does not require an application or contribution agreement. If the founder identifies work as intended for commercial incorporation into Aniob, the ownership terms will be shared with the contributor **before that work starts**. Accepted work intended for incorporation is covered by a separate written contribution agreement, including any agreed copyright assignment. The contributor can review the terms, ask questions, and decide whether to proceed. The PR itself is not the agreement.

Contributors retain ownership of their pre-existing intellectual property unless they separately agree otherwise in writing. A contribution agreement should clearly identify what work it covers. Accepted work will be credited in the project's contributors list or release acknowledgements, respecting the contributor's preferred attribution.

## Third-party materials

Libraries and other third-party materials keep their own owners and original licences or terms. A contributor must identify third-party code, assets, models, or data they include and confirm they are permitted to contribute them. A contribution agreement does not change a third party's licence.

This policy is a plain-language project summary, not a contribution agreement or legal advice. Do not share sensitive identity documents or unnecessary personal information as part of onboarding.
